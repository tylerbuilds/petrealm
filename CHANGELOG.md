# Changes

## 0.6 — The little trails

- Three original paths, each with three rotating encounter sequences. Make three choices with your companion to complete an outing; approaches change reactions and the final discovery.
- Each friend earns their own bond. Glowpool opens at two bond, their personality knack at three, and Windridge at four. The knack adds a real encounter choice.
- Find nine original treasures, read location hints in the field journal, and display three different finds in the meadow. Moving or replacing a display keeps the treasure yours.
- Outings save between choices and resume after reload. Repeat finds still earn bond, happiness and XP, without pebble payouts, daily resets, streak pressure or waiting timers.
- Strict version 3 saves migrate accepted earlier worlds without changing their existing money, companions or reward histories. The current version 1 original is retained before migration; the first version 2 original and earlier recovery backup stay protected during the version 2 upgrade, including its guided collision recovery.
- The existing care, two games, wardrobes, growth, bank, quest, Lodge and arena systems remain available. Original companion artwork is retained.

These are original local gameplay additions. They do not add online accounts, multiplayer, trading, a server economy or full Neopets service parity. Browser, platform and hosted qualification is recorded in [release verification](docs/verification.md).
