# Changes

## 0.8: A friend with stories of their own

- A personal field journal, free asynchronous expeditions to three places, authored return stories and meadow keepsakes that never expire.
- Curiosity, kindness and courage grow through care and exploration, changing greetings and biography while preserving each friend’s original identity.
- Time away no longer reduces care. Seven completed quest days earn a bonus without consecutive-day pressure.
- A calmer home invitation, clear outing choices, a welcome after longer breaks, and mobile journal layouts down to 320px.
- Offline illustrated biography and SVG portrait downloads. Each journal keeps its latest three stories and lifetime outing count; finds remain collected.
- Memory meadow is untimed by default, with a timed challenge available. Keyboard focus and card announcements are improved.
- Validated older-save upgrades, atomic expedition claims, cross-tab protection and compact saves for fully populated worlds.


## 0.6: The little trails

- Three paths, each with three changing sets of encounters. Make three choices with your friend to finish an outing. Your approach changes their reaction and the final find.
- Each friend builds their own bond. Glowpool opens at two bond, their knack at three, and Windridge at four. The knack gives them another way to handle an encounter.
- Find nine treasures, read the journal’s location hints and show three different finds in the meadow. Moving or replacing a display keeps the treasure yours.
- Outings save between choices, so you can resume after a reload. Finding a treasure again still earns bond, Happiness and XP. Trails pay no pebbles and have no daily resets or waiting timers.
- Version 3 saves accept valid older worlds and keep their money, friends and claimed rewards. Before upgrading, Petrealm keeps the current version 1 original. It also protects the first version 2 original and earlier recovery backup. If another version 2 original is already kept, Save helps you download the current one and finish the upgrade safely.
- Care, both games, outfits, bloom, banking, quests, Lodge and arena still work, with the same original creature art.

These are Petrealm’s own local game additions. There are no online accounts, multiplayer, trading or server economy, and no claim to reproduce all of Neopets. [Checks by release](docs/verification.md) lists the browser, platform and hosted tests.
