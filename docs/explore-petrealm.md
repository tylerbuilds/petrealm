# Let’s explore Petrealm

Meet a friend, make a little home, then see where the paths take you. Petrealm is an original creature game. There are nine animal and monster companions, and every one can play, dress up, train and grow.

## Your first adventure

1. Pick a companion, choose their personality and give them a name. Press **Let’s go!** to enter Sunmeadow.
2. Try a **Berry treat**, **Acorn treat**, **Ribbon play** or **Cosy nap**. Fullness, Happiness and Energy show how your friend feels. Snacks are used up; the ribbon can be used again.
3. Try **Trails** for a three-choice outing, a discovery and a closer bond. **Show in meadow** brings your find home. [The little trails](little-trails.md) explains routes, choices and costs.
4. Follow another meadow sign, or choose a place at the top of the screen. **Map & quests** shows every destination and your adventure notebook.

Mallow the owl has a few ideas when you need one. You choose what to do next. There is no account to create or payment to make.

## Places to visit

| Place | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Meadow** | Share treats, play, nap, choose another friend, try an outfit and mark a first bloom. |
| **Market** | Meet Juniper, spend earned pebbles on snacks and treasures, or put pebbles in the Pebble Keep. |
| **Games** | Catch the firefly in the golden patch, or turn over flower cards and find six matching pairs. |
| **Map & quests** | Choose a path, try today’s quests, collect the gifts you earned and visit the Explorer Lodge. |
| **Little trails** | Make three choices with your friend, find treasures, unlock their knack and decorate the meadow. |
| **Training** | Practise with your companion, then play turn-by-turn matches against friendly rivals. |

The Pebble Keep sign on the map takes you straight to the bank in Market lane. Deposit and withdraw whole pebbles. Interest is a game reward: collect it yourself once each UTC day.

## Your satchel and friends

Open **Satchel**, or the meadow’s **Mail & gifts** button, to see your snacks, outfits and supplies. Mallow’s daily letter contains 40 pebbles. Adventure keepsakes remember things you have already tried.

**Try on an outfit** takes you to Dress up in the meadow. A leaf cap adds two attack in a match; a moon scarf blocks two damage. Taking an accessory off lets another friend wear it.

The **Friends** button opens your family. Choose a companion to play with, or adopt another. Each friend keeps their own care, outfit, experience and bloom. Finish a match before changing friends or outfits. Finish or leave a saved trail before choosing another friend.

## Growing together

Care, finished games, quest gifts and matches earn XP. Every 100 XP raises a level, up to level 20. Practice costs 20 pebbles, 12 Energy and 4 Fullness, and gives 18 XP.

A friend’s first bloom needs level 3, a meal, ribbon play and a nap shared with that same friend. Open the bloom story in the meadow to see what is ready. Blooming costs 60 pebbles once, gives a new look, up to 40 XP, 10 Happiness and 10 Energy, and makes future practice give 24 XP. Stats stop at 100.

Each companion has a personality. Some new species also have a favourite snack. The adoption preview and care hint explain their small bonuses. [Meet every companion and see the exact bonuses](companions.md).

## Play again another day

Your world saves automatically in this browser. Come back to the same browser to keep playing with the same friends. **Save → Download save file** keeps a copy you can move to another device. Restore a save through the same menu; your previous world is kept as a recovery backup.

If the screen says your world is unsaved, download a save before closing it. Petrealm has no online account or shared economy. Linux and Mac can both open the same save file.

Quests, mail gifts and game pebble limits refresh at midnight UTC. Finished games can still earn happiness and XP after the 120-pebble daily game limit. Seven consecutive daily quest-completion gifts earn a bonus; a missed day begins a fresh trail. There is no need to keep a streak to explore or play.

## Keyboard and smaller screens

Use Tab to move between labelled controls and Enter or Space to choose one. Press Space or **Catch the light** in the firefly game. Escape closes the satchel and save menus. The map’s phone signposts sit beneath its illustration so every destination has room. Decorative motion follows your device’s reduced-motion setting.
