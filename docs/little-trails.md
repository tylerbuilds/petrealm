# The little trails

Follow the **Trails** sign in your meadow, or choose **Go on a trail** beside Mallow. Pick a path, make three choices with your friend, and bring a little wonder home.

Whisperwood is open from the start. Two bond opens Glowpool; four bond opens Windridge. Each friend opens their own paths. You never have to keep a streak, wait for a timer or pay pebbles to explore.

## Pick your way through

Setting out uses **6 Energy and 2 Fullness**. Your friend needs at least **10 Energy and 2 Fullness** to begin. Share a snack or nap in the meadow when you need one.

Each outing has three encounters. Lend a paw, notice something small, follow a new path or try something bold. The next scene remembers your choice. The final approach changes what you find.

Finished outings give up to **12 XP**, up to **10 Happiness**, and **1 bond** with that friend. Choose an approach your friend enjoys at least once to gain **2 bond** instead:

| Personality | Favourite approach |
| --- | --- |
| Steady | Notice small things |
| Gentle | Lend a paw |
| Curious | Follow their curiosity |
| Spirited | Try something bold |

At **3 bond**, your friend offers a third choice: their own knack. It is a new way to answer an encounter, counts as their favourite approach and can change the final discovery.

Come back to a route for a different story. Each route has three encounter sequences, cycling through your friend's completed outings. There are no wrong answers or losing encounters.

## Bring a wonder home

Each finished outing finds one of nine original treasures. Open **Your field journal** to see what you have found and hints for the others. Finding an old favourite still earns bond and XP, even after the mini-games' daily pebble limit.

Choose **Show in meadow**, then pick **By the pond**, **At the treehouse** or **Beside the path**. Your discovery appears in your meadow. Moving or putting away a treasure keeps it in the journal; replacing one never loses it. Three different finds can be shown at once.

These are meadow decorations, not shop supplies or arena equipment. Outings do not pay pebbles, consume inventory or advance daily quests. Your existing game rewards and prices stay the same.

## Stop and return whenever you like

Every choice saves. Visit another place, open your satchel or close the browser; return through **Resume trail** to continue the same outing. Care, games, shopping and practice remain available. Finish or leave your outing before switching friends, adopting or starting an arena match.

**Head home early** ends an unfinished outing. The 6 Energy and 2 Fullness used to set out stay spent; it earns no discovery, XP, happiness or bond. There is no extra fee. Your earlier finds and bond remain.

## Older worlds

Accepted older saves keep every existing companion, balance, outfit and reward history. Everyone starts with zero trail bond; Petrealm does not invent past outings. New files use save version 3. Earlier Petrealm versions reject them, so keep an original if you want to return to an older release.

The first original version 2 file stays protected in the browser and can be downloaded from **Save**. If another older world needs upgrading while that original is already protected, Petrealm keeps both earlier local backups and leaves the current world unsaved. Download **this world's original version 2 save**, check that you have the file, then choose **I've kept the original · finish upgrade**. This finishes saving your current progress without replacing either earlier backup. Storage or other-tab conflicts leave your originals unchanged and your unsaved world exportable.
