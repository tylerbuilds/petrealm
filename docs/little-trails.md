# The little trails

Follow the **Trails** sign in your meadow, or choose **Go on a trail** beside Mallow. Pick a path, make three choices with your friend and bring a treasure home.

Whisperwood is open from the start. Reach 2 bond to open Glowpool and 4 bond to open Windridge. Each friend opens their own paths. Exploring costs Energy and Fullness, but no pebbles. There’s no timer or streak to keep.

## Pick your way through

Setting out uses **6 Energy and 2 Fullness**. Your friend needs at least **10 Energy and 2 Fullness** to begin. Share a snack or nap in the meadow when you need one.

Each outing has three encounters. Lend a paw, notice something small, follow a new path or try something bold. The next scene remembers your choice. The final approach changes what you find.

Finished outings give up to **12 XP**, up to **10 Happiness**, and **1 bond** with that friend. Choose an approach your friend enjoys at least once to gain **2 bond** instead:

| Personality | Favourite approach |
| --- | --- |
| Steady | Notice small things |
| Gentle | Lend a paw |
| Curious | Follow their curiosity |
| Spirited | Try something bold |

At **3 bond**, your friend offers a third choice called their **knack**. It’s a special way through the trail, counts as their favourite approach and can change the treasure you find.

Come back to a route for a different story. Each route has three stories. Finishing outings moves your friend through them in turn. There are no wrong answers or losing encounters.

## Bring a wonder home

Each finished outing finds one of nine original treasures. Open **Your field journal** to see what you have found and hints for the others. Finding an old favourite still earns bond and XP, even after the mini-games' daily pebble limit.

Choose **Show in meadow**, then pick **By the pond**, **At the treehouse** or **Beside the path**. Your discovery appears in your meadow. Moving or putting away a treasure keeps it in the journal; replacing one never loses it. Three different finds can be shown at once.

Trail treasures decorate the meadow. You can’t spend them or use them in matches. Outings earn XP, Happiness and bond, but no pebbles or daily quest progress. They don’t use any of your supplies.

## Take a break

Petrealm saves each choice. Visit another place, open your satchel or close the browser, then choose **Resume trail** to carry on. You can still care, play games, shop and practise during a trail. Finish or leave it before switching friends, adopting or starting a match.

**Head home early** ends an unfinished outing. You won’t get back the 6 Energy and 2 Fullness used to start. It earns no treasure, XP, Happiness or bond, and there’s no extra fee. You keep your earlier finds and bond.

## Older worlds

Older saves keep your friends, pebbles, outfits and collected gifts. Each friend starts with zero trail bond. New saves use version 3, which earlier Petrealm releases can’t open. Keep an original file if you want to return to an older release.

Petrealm keeps the first original version 2 save in this browser. You can download it from **Save**. If another older world needs upgrading, the game may ask you to keep a copy before continuing. Download **this world’s original version 2 save**, check that you have the file, then choose **I’ve kept the original · finish upgrade**. Your earlier original and backup stay here. If saving fails or another tab changes the world, you can still download your unsaved progress.
