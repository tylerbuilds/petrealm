# From clicking through to playing

The previous version passed its interaction checks, but Tyler's playtest found that it was not fun: most actions advanced text or started a waiting period. The clarified audience is roughly **8–18**. This revision changes the core activity, rather than adding more dialogue choices.

## What you can do now

Choose a Rillum and go straight into Broadroot Rush. Move with **WASD or arrow keys**, press **Space to dash**, or use the touch movement pad and Dash button. Tapping a clear destination also moves your companion along a navigable route.

Collect at least five of the eight glowseeds, then reach the neighbour at the glowing arch. Moving brambles, muddy crossings and pulsing ripples make timing and route choice matter. A collision briefly slows the companion; there are no lives to lose. The clock counts up and never ends the run.

| Trail | Play | First completion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broadroot Rush | Weave through fallen roots, dash past moving brambles and look for a hidden hollow. | Fern arch; opens Brook Skip. |
| Brook Skip | Find the safe crossings between water channels and avoid moving mud. | Skipping stones; opens Lantern Trail. |
| Lantern Trail | Follow the shore, time dashes through ripples and find the warm-root flowers. | Golden petals. |

Each trail awards a star for finishing, another for all eight seeds and another for beating its target time. Secrets and personal bests persist. Gentler obstacles enlarge the pickup area and ease the hazards, with the same rewards and progression.

## Why come back?

The immediate loop is **move → collect → dodge → finish → build**. Seeds collected during actual play pay for moss, reeds and a pebble pool. First-completion rewards are free and appear at home automatically. Built decorations can be swapped without paying again. Replaying lets you improve your route, earn missing stars or find a secret.

The three home locations are the roots, canopy and riverside. Earned objects appear in the home scene; they are not just an entry in an inventory. Stories, discovered finds and the quieter walks remain available, but completing their dialogue is not required to play.

## Pauses, saves and old worlds

Switching to Home or another in-app page pauses and preserves the current in-memory run. Return to Play and choose **Keep exploring**. Pausing, leaving the browser tab and opening Save stop the clock. Restarting a trail is free. Reloading the page starts an unfinished trail again; completed discoveries and home changes are saved.

A run receives a saved identifier before play. Its result can be collected once, even if another tab tries to collect it. A replacement world cannot receive a previous world's result. Failed storage writes keep the current visit exportable. Explicit save errors retain the result and offer another save attempt.

Version 1 Under the Hedge saves migrate to version 2 by adding empty play progress. Existing names, appearances, stories, finds, neighbours and prepared walks are retained. The original stored bytes remain until the next successful save. Importing still retains the previous world as a backup. The separate previous Petrealm game remains available.

## What this revision establishes

This is a concrete response to the reported lack of play: direct control, visible feedback, obstacle timing, exploration, short goals and usable rewards. It is not evidence that all children and teenagers will enjoy it equally. The next useful playtest is whether players can start unaided, choose to retry a trail and use an earned reward at home. A broad 8–18 audience needs observation across ages; automated checks establish functionality, not enjoyment.

There are three fixed course layouts, with moving obstacles. This is not a procedural open world. No online accounts, multiplayer, purchases, advertising, tracking or external runtime services were added.
