# Under the Hedge art production

Production date: **10 October 2026**. Creative direction: Tyler’s supplied *Under the Hedge: research and development recommendation*. The user authorised generation of assets for this product. The direction calls for an illustrated, creature-scale countryside with readable silhouettes, selective texture and homes shaped by non-human bodies.

## Original editable creatures

Source: [`src/hedge-art.js`](../../src/hedge-art.js). These illustrations were authored directly as SVG paths and pose transforms for this project. They were not generated by a raster image model, traced from existing characters or supplied by an external character-art package. There is no image-generation prompt for these vector illustrations.

The exported `familyCreature(family, options)` returns a complete accessible SVG. It uses a `320 × 260` viewBox, escaped labels, fixed illustration paths and allowlisted appearance settings. It has no shared SVG IDs, external image references, filters or remote fonts. Individual portraits can be repeated within a page or exported independently.

### Anatomy brief used to author the vectors

| Family | Large shapes and expression | Individual details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rillum** | A low pear body, two small forepaws, two broad webbed hindfeet, paddle tail and pleated cheek sensory folds. The folds are continuous pleats, not external gill stalks. Muted clay, stone and river colours; belly, speckle and tail variation. | Mallow has a broader paddle, uneven pale speckling and a differently held forepaw. |
| **Tuffet** | A compressed dome, four short visible feet and broad folding ears. A resting body and asymmetrical ear positions carry expression. | Dindle’s ears fold unevenly, and a small moss patch sits in its outer coat. |
| **Limmer** | A narrow low body, four climbing feet, folding side fans and a long balancing tail ending in a small light-bearing tuft. The body itself does not glow. | Pell has dark forefeet and a pale fan edge. Ossel has a creased fan, tilted head and untidy tuft. |

The Rillum has seven named poses: `greet`, `investigate`, `choose`, `hesitate`, `settle`, `listen` and `familiar`. The pose system changes body, head, forepaw, cheek-fold and tail transforms together with the eyes. The other families reuse the pose state within their own anatomy. These are pose variants, not a claim of a completed frame-by-frame animation set.

The renderer supports `clay`, `stone`, `river`, `water` and `reed` palette identifiers; `water` is another muted water palette. The player-facing companion choices are river, clay and stone. Marking options are `speckles`, `belly` and `tail`. No palette is presented as a rare or superior companion.

`hedgeIcon(name)` supplies a separate set of small stroke-based SVG interface marks. Existing Petrealm illustration files were retained for the previous world.

## Generated environment paintings

The two final environment images were created with the built-in **imagegen** tool, then revised through imagegen editing. The tool did not return a model identifier. No model/version is inferred here. Both final scenes are **1536 × 1024**; they contain environment art without player characters, text labels or embedded interface controls.

The first passes were considered too photographic. The final editing direction simplified surfaces into a more visibly illustrated watercolour/gouache treatment, with coloured outlines, painted foliage and selective detail. Creature portraits and interactive home elements remain separate SVG/UI layers so that the raster background does not determine the companion’s appearance or stored state.

### Reconstructed production briefs

**The original exact tool prompts were not retained in the available handover.** The following are reconstructed briefs from the production record, supplied direction and final images. They are not quoted tool inputs and should not be treated as exact reproducibility records.

**Bramble Fold — reconstructed scene brief**

> An original illustrated home beneath an English hedgerow, seen at small-creature height. A sheltered, unfurnished hollow opens under intertwined roots on the left, with a dry earth threshold and enough quiet foreground space to place a companion. A shallow brook runs along the right, bordered by stones, reeds, fallen leaves and moss. Thorn flowers and broad leaves make an overhead canopy; soft countryside is visible beyond. Warm ochre earth, moss and hazel greens, muted blue-green water. Watercolour/gouache surfaces, restrained drawn edges and selective natural texture. No people, dressed animals, furniture, signs, typography or interface. Keep the home and foreground readable.

**Alderwater — reconstructed scene brief**

> An original illustrated lake vista from the low near shore. Alder roots, moss, reeds and a dry shore path frame calm water, with wooded English countryside beyond. A small sheltered root hollow across the lake contains a restrained cluster of warm flowers. The light should be a discoverable distant detail associated with a warm seep, not glaring fantasy magic or a grand portal. Muted river colours, warm late light and a hand-painted watercolour/gouache surface. Clear large shapes, selective foliage detail, no characters, text, buildings or interface. The shore route should be visible as a possible way around the water.

**Final style revision — reconstructed edit brief**

> Preserve the scene composition and useful open spaces while reducing photographic surface detail. Use simplified painterly shapes, softer watercolour/gouache washes, selective drawn contours and coherent restrained colour. Keep natural materials recognisable. Maintain the small warm flower clue at Alderwater without exaggerating its glow.

The final Bramble Fold image includes a fallen seed-case-like shape beside the water. That painted element is environmental set dressing; collected finds and their placement are represented separately by game state and interface layers.

## Review boundaries

The environment paintings are generated illustrations, and the creatures are original code-authored vectors. The working title and family names retain the screening limitations in the supplied research. No completed trademark clearance, comparative character-similarity review or audience association study is implied by this production record.

The useful next art reviews are small silhouettes without colour, expressions without dialogue, companion contrast over both backgrounds and readability on narrow screens. Runtime and browser results should be reported in release QA receipts, separately from the production provenance here.
